VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: A stranger reading this plan in five seconds sees "quit job, sell candles, $8,000, 40 orders" and cannot tell whether that is a living or a hobby, because the one number that would make it clear (what a month of sales earns against what a month of life costs) is missing.
DECIDING FACT: The record gives no prices, margins, monthly costs, or time period for the 40 orders, and the defense concedes it "cannot show that the business will cover the user's living costs, or how long $8,000 will last."
WOULD FLIP IF: The case stated, in one line, monthly candle profit and monthly living costs showing the $8,000 covers the gap long enough for the shop to pay its own way.
