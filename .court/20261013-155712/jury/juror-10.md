VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: I would not put my own money into a plan that gives up a known salary for a business where I can't see the price, the margin, the monthly burn, or how long it took to get the sales, because I can't measure either the upside or how much I could lose.
DECIDING FACT: The defense concedes the record has no prices, margins, monthly costs, time period for the 40 orders, or current salary, so nobody can work out how long the $8,000 lasts or whether 40 orders could ever replace an income.
WOULD FLIP IF: The case showed the 40 orders came in a recent short period at a margin that, scaled to full time, covers the user's stated monthly costs within the months the $8,000 can pay for.
