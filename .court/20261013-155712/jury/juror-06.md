VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: I would not take a system live without load numbers, and this plan cuts the only stable power supply (the salary) before anyone knows the throughput (orders per month), the margin per unit, or the burn rate that the $8,000 has to cover.
DECIDING FACT: The record gives no time period, prices, margins or monthly costs for the 40 orders and $8,000, and the defense concedes it "cannot show that the business will cover the user's living costs, or how long $8,000 will last."
WOULD FLIP IF: The case showed a recent monthly order rate and per-candle margin whose profit, plus the $8,000 spread over the user's stated monthly costs, covers living expenses for a defined runway of several months.
