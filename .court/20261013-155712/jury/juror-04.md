VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: A plan to give up a salary carries the burden of showing it can replace that salary, and this record offers only 40 orders of unknown timing and margin and $8,000 of unknown runway, which is hope dressed up as fact, not proof.
DECIDING FACT: The defense conceded that without prices, margins, monthly costs, the time period of the 40 orders, or current salary, it "cannot show that the business will cover the user's living costs, or how long $8,000 will last."
WOULD FLIP IF: The record showed monthly living and business costs alongside per-order profit and the time period of the 40 orders, and the arithmetic showed current sales (or a documented growth rate) covering those costs before the $8,000 runs out.
