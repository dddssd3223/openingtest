VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: As a parent I would not let anyone in my family give up a paycheck on $8,000 of savings and 40 orders when nobody, not even the defense, can say how many months that money buys or whether the candles turn a profit.
DECIDING FACT: The record gives no monthly costs, prices, margins or current salary, and the defense concedes it "cannot show that the business will cover the user's living costs, or how long $8,000 will last."
WOULD FLIP IF: The record showed monthly living and business costs alongside per-order profit and sales rate, proving the shop already covers those costs or that $8,000 covers a realistic ramp-up period.
