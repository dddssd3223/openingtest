VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: If someone pitched me "quit your job for 40 candle orders," I wouldn't buy that plan this week: I can't tell what a candle costs, what it earns, or how many months $8,000 lasts, so I'd keep my paycheck and keep selling on the side.
DECIDING FACT: The record gives no time period, prices, margins or monthly costs for the "Sales so far: 40 orders" and "$8,000" figures, and the defense concedes it "cannot show that the business will cover the user's living costs, or how long $8,000 will last."
WOULD FLIP IF: The record showed that recent monthly candle profit, at a stated price and margin, already roughly covers monthly living costs, with $8,000 covering several months of any shortfall.
