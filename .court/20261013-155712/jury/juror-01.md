VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: I can't sign off on giving up a paycheck when nobody can tell me the price, the margin, the monthly burn or the salary being lost, so there is no way to know whether $8,000 covers two months or twelve.
DECIDING FACT: The record gives $8,000 in savings but "no information on prices, margins, monthly costs, time period of the 40 sales, repeat customers, or current salary," and the defense concedes it "cannot show that the business will cover the user's living costs, or how long $8,000 will last."
WOULD FLIP IF: The case showed monthly net profit from the candle sales, over a stated period, that covers the user's monthly living costs, with $8,000 left as a buffer of several months on top.
