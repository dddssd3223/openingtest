# Opening Statement: Prosecution

The charge: the plan should not go ahead as described. The user's plan is "quit their current job and sell handmade candles on Etsy full time." Here is why it fails as stated.

**1. Forty orders do not show that the shop can pay a full-time income.**
The whole sales record is "Sales so far: 40 orders." The record says nothing about the "time period of the 40 sales" or about "prices" and "margins." Forty orders could have come in one month or over three years, and the profit on each could be large or close to zero. Nobody can tell from this whether candles would replace a salary. Cost if I am right: the user gives up a known income for a revenue stream that may not cover rent.

**2. Nobody has worked out how long $8,000 will last.**
"Savings set aside: $8,000" is the only cushion. With no "monthly costs" in the record, nobody can say how many months that money buys. Inventory, wax, shipping and Etsy fees come out of the same pot as living expenses. Cost if I am right: the user runs out of savings before sales are steady, and the business fails because cash ran out, even if demand was real.

**3. Quitting is the most expensive step, and it comes first.**
The plan starts with quitting the job. The record gives no "current salary," so the user's own figures do not even show what they are giving up. The 40 orders came while the user was still employed, which shows the shop can run part-time. Nothing in the record says why the job has to end before the shop proves it can grow. Cost if I am right: the user loses a salary that is hard to win back, when keeping the job while testing would have cost little.

**4. There is no evidence of repeat buyers or of growth.**
The record has no information on "repeat customers." Forty sales could be friends, family, or one-time buyers. Without repeat buyers, every sale has to be won again from strangers, with no shown way to grow. Cost if I am right: sales stay flat at a hobby level while the user's costs are now full-time.

**What the record lacks:** price, margin, monthly costs, how long the 40 sales took, repeat-buyer rate, and current salary. A full-time plan needs every one of these, and none of them was given.
